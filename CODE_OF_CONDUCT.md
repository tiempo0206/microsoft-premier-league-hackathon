# Code of Conduct

Our team follows the conduct expectations in the [hackathon's official Code of Conduct](https://github.com/microsoft/insidethegamehackathon/blob/main/CODE%20OF%20CONDUCT.md).

Use respectful and inclusive language, give constructive feedback, and respect teammates' privacy. Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable in Issues, pull requests, and team communication.

For event-related concerns, follow the reporting process in the official document. For repository collaboration concerns, contact the repository owner privately.

The conduct document covers community behavior. Competition requirements are published separately in the [Official Rules](https://github.com/microsoft/insidethegamehackathon/blob/main/OFFICIAL%20RULES.md).
